● Ongoing — Thesis

Multi-Objective University Course Scheduling Using Flexible Job Shop Problem (FJSP) Formulation

Undergraduate Thesis · Department of IPE, RUET · Supervisor: Khairun Nahar

This thesis models the university course scheduling problem for the IPE department at RUET as a Flexible Job Shop Problem (FJSP) — a well-known NP-hard combinatorial optimization problem. The scheduling problem involves assigning courses to faculty members, rooms, and time slots while satisfying hard constraints (no conflicts, room capacity, faculty availability) and optimizing multiple soft objectives simultaneously.

Two solution approaches are implemented and benchmarked: (1) NSGA-II (Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II) — a population-based metaheuristic for generating Pareto-optimal solutions, and (2) Gurobi MIP — an exact solver providing provably optimal benchmarks for small-scale instances. The work includes scalability studies, idle gap constraint modeling, and a FastAPI + Chart.js web application for real-time schedule generation with Excel output.

● Under Review — Journal Manuscript

NLP-Based Occupational Hazard Classification in Manufacturing Using Sentence Transformers, BERT, FUCOM, and RPN

Submitted to Discover Artificial Intelligence (Springer Nature) · May 2026

This manuscript applies Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to classify occupational hazards in manufacturing environments. Sentence Transformers and BERT-based models are used to semantically embed and categorize hazard descriptions from unstructured text data. The Full Consistency Method (FUCOM) is applied for multi-criteria weighting of hazard dimensions, while Risk Priority Numbers (RPN) from FMEA are used to quantify and rank hazard severity.

The integrated NLP-FUCOM-RPN framework enables automated, scalable hazard identification — reducing reliance on manual expert assessment and improving occupational safety decision-making in manufacturing plants.

Operations Research & Optimization

Linear, nonlinear, and integer programming (LP, MIP, MILP, MINLP), stochastic programming, game theory, and scheduling theory. Tools include Gurobi, CPLEX, and custom Python solvers.

🤖

Machine Learning & Deep Learning

Supervised and unsupervised learning, ensemble methods (SVM, Random Forest), neural networks, and reinforcement learning. Applied to healthcare, manufacturing, and supply chain domains.

🔤

Natural Language Processing

Text classification, semantic similarity, and information extraction using transformer-based models (BERT, Sentence Transformers). Applied to hazard classification and document analysis.

🏭

Supply Chain Optimization

Inventory management, demand forecasting, EOQ modeling, and resilience strategies. Focus on pharmaceutical and garments supply chains in developing economies like Bangladesh.

📅

Scheduling Theory

Combinatorial scheduling problems modeled as Job Shop, Flexible Job Shop, and Flow Shop Problems. Multi-objective formulations solved via exact (MIP) and metaheuristic (NSGA-II, GA) methods.

⚠️

Risk Management & Safety

Occupational hazard identification, FMEA, RPN-based risk prioritization, and ergonomic risk assessment using RULA, REBA, and OWAS methods in industrial environments.
